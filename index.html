INGREDIENTS

QuietTone Ingredients: What the Artwork Lists

The product artwork names six ingredients but shows no Supplement Facts panel, so amounts and full contents must be confirmed on the official label.

B6

Vitamin B6

An essential vitamin involved in normal metabolism and nervous system function. Very high long-term intakes can cause problems, so dose matters.

Zn

Zinc

An essential mineral. Compare the dose with your total daily intake from food and other supplements.

Ash

Ashwagandha

A traditional herb used in stress-related wellness products. It may not suit everyone, including some people who are pregnant or have thyroid conditions.

Cha

Chamomile

A botanical common in teas and relaxation products. People with ragweed-family allergies should be careful.

GABA

GABA

A naturally occurring amino acid. Evidence that oral GABA acts in the brain is limited.

Bac

Bacopa

A herb from Ayurvedic tradition, studied mainly for memory. It can cause digestive upset in some people.

General ingredient information only, not claims about QuietTone's performance. The list comes from supplied artwork and may differ from the current label.

What to verify on the label

A trustworthy supplement label is specific. Look for:

  • Amount per serving for every ingredient
  • Whether a proprietary blend hides individual doses
  • Allergen and other-ingredients statements
  • Third-party testing or manufacturing standards

What is QuietTone?

A capsule dietary supplement presented as ear wellness support. Check the current label for its precise description.

Does QuietTone treat tinnitus or hearing loss?

The supplied materials include no clinical trial data on the finished product. Ask the seller for any published evidence.

What ingredients are in it?

The artwork names vitamin B6, zinc, ashwagandha, chamomile, GABA and bacopa. Confirm amounts on the Supplement Facts panel.

There is no verified timeframe. Be sceptical of anyone who promises one.
